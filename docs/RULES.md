# Rules

The day follow spindle GMT-3, and the week starts at Sunday.

## Users

- Only group members can register, using a command in the group chat
- Who left the group cant use the system, but him register stay

## Habits

- Each habit can be completed once a day.
- Just the Owner of the group can create habits
- Habit has a category, points per completion and name
- Habits could be edited and disabled, the points earned stay
- Habit's conclusion can undone just in the same day of the conclusion

## Ranking

There're two rankings: daily and weekly.
Both based on habits completion during the period.

## Future

On the final of the period, the top 3 will gain additional points
